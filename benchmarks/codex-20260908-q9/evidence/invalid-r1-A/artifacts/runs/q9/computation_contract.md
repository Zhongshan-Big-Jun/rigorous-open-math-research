# Computation contract

Object: numerical tuples satisfying C1-C3 approximately. C3 solved monotonically for g, C2 solved monotonically for m, C1 scanned in B. Score: Q>0 and small/negative R; invalid domain rejects candidate. Deterministic grid; no seed. Double precision, evidence only. Initial budget 60 seconds. Blind spots: thin branches, tangencies, boundaries, floating rounding. Any refutation needs exact existence proof with rigorous sign bounds.
