# Transformed supplied-bound deficit
Let a=H_m(A), b=H_m(B), p=tan(a), q=tan(b), and S=c²-r-k(1-c²)sin²A. On the original acute domain,
sin²A=p²/(m²+p²), sin²B=q²/(m²+q²), and
r=p²(m²+q²)/[q²(m²+p²)]. Direct substitution gives
S=m²/[m²+p²] [c²-p²/q²-(1-c²)p²].
In particular, under C1, b=(1-c)pi+ca and the sign of this expression is independent of m after (c,a) are fixed. This is an algebraic identity, not a proof of Q9.
